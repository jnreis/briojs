# Community conduct

Brio welcomes questions, feedback, and contributions from people of all
backgrounds and experience levels. You do not need to be an expert to take part.

- Be respectful. Critique ideas and code, not the person behind them.
- Be patient with questions, mistakes, and differences of opinion.
- Give specific, constructive feedback. Disagreement is welcome; personal
  attacks, harassment, discriminatory remarks, and threats are not.
- Respect privacy. Do not share someone else's personal information without
  permission.
- Keep discussions relevant. Do not spam or pressure people to contribute,
  promote, or respond.

These expectations apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, and
Discussions, and other Brio community spaces managed by the maintainer.

## Reporting a problem

Contact the repository maintainer privately at **jonathan@briojs.com** (the
project contact listed in the package metadata). Include links and enough
context to understand what happened. Do not post private reports publicly.
For software vulnerabilities, use the separate [security policy](./SECURITY.md).

The maintainer will review reports as time permits and may ask for changes,
remove content, restrict participation, or block an account depending on the
severity and pattern of behavior. The same expectations apply to the maintainer.
